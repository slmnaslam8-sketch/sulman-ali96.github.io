About

I'm a Lahore-based content writer with over nine years of experience across digital media, broadcast journalism, and corporate learning. My work spans automobile coverage for Pakistan's largest auto platform, political analysis for the Stimson Centre's South Asian Voices, breaking news for national TV, and instructional content for a global e-learning company.

I hold an M.Phil. in International Relations from the University of Punjab, which grounds my writing in careful research and analytical depth — whether I'm reviewing an SUV or unpacking geopolitical dynamics. I've taught International Law, published peer-reviewed research, and contributed a chapter to a book on Pakistan's national security.

Currently at KnowledgeCity, I develop instructional scripts for professional development courses spanning Business, Finance, and Compliance — translating complex concepts into engaging, learner-focused content aligned with adult learning principles.
